# 2.15.10 — build 1

The share image, plus the brand icons.

## Share image
- **Every lift is on the Session card.** It kept four and dropped the rest,
  so a six-exercise session shared as four. Every lift with a working set
  is listed now, in session order, as **sets-weight×reps** (`4-225×6`)
  — the same order as the LAST WK chip. The day label and hero number gave
  up a little height to pay for it, the week/phase line grew, and rows
  scale to the count: five at full size, six at about 90%, eight at about
  65%.
- **New Quote style.** The quote from that workout's complete screen, set
  large with the author under it, and the session's sets, reps and time at
  the bottom. Offered on every session, so the style picker is always
  visible now.
- **The logo on the card is the forged F**, the App Store icon. It was the
  old PWA glyph.

## Brand
- The web favicon, PWA home-screen icon and Apple touch icon are the forged
  F, regenerated from the App Store artwork. The old crucible glyphs and a
  dead canvas favicon generator are removed.
