Getting started

The Foundry builds you a mesocycle — a multi-week training block that progresses on purpose rather than at random. Answer a few questions about your goal, experience, available days, and equipment, and it generates a program you can start the same day.

You don't need an account to try it. Training is stored on your device immediately; creating an account is what syncs it across devices and backs it up.

Common questions

Why is it suggesting fewer reps than I did last week?

It shouldn't. Every exercise carries your last session forward and adds to it — weight on loaded lifts, reps on bodyweight movements. If you see a target lower than what you actually hit, email us with the exercise name and the week, and we'll look at it.

I added a set. Will it stick?

Yes. Adding or removing a set is remembered and carries into the following weeks, on top of whatever the program prescribes. If the program adds a set on its own, you'll get that too. Remove it any time and that becomes the new normal.

Can I swap an exercise?

Tap the exercise, then Swap. You can pick from the exercise library or add your own. Your history follows the exercise, not the slot, so swapping doesn't lose anything.

Can I reorder my workout?

Yes — and when you do, you'll be asked whether the new order applies to today only or to the rest of the mesocycle.

How do I train with a friend?

Share your invite code from the Friends tab. Once they join, you're both on the same program and can see each other's completed sessions. Your logs stay your own — swapping or reordering on your side never changes their program.

Apple Health

Turn it on in Settings → Apple Health. iOS will ask two separate permissions, and you can allow one and refuse the other:

  • Body weight — reads your latest reading so you don't retype it, and saves the ones you log back to Health.
  • Workouts — saves each finished session so it appears in Apple Fitness and counts toward your Activity rings.

Calories are estimated on your device from session length and body weight. If you wear an Apple Watch, its measurement is more accurate and takes precedence. You can revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → The Foundry.

Account and data

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The reset link arrives by email — check spam if it doesn't show up within a few minutes.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated training data. You can also email us to request the same.

What data do you collect?

Your account email, your training log, and body weight if you choose to record it. No advertising identifiers, no location, no third-party analytics trackers. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Reporting a bug

Email timberandcode3@gmail.com. The most useful things to include:

  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The exercise, day, and week if it's about a specific session.
  • Your device and iOS version, or your browser if you're on the web.
  • A screenshot, if it's something you can see.
