The Foundry

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Who we are

The Foundry is a training app developed by Timber & Code Columbus Collective. Contact: timberandcode3@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account info: email and password (if you create an account). Passwords are hashed by our auth provider — we never see or store plaintext passwords.
  • Training data: your workouts, sets, reps, weights, exercise history, program configuration, and workout notes. Tied to your account.
  • Body metrics: body weight — the readings you enter in the app, plus readings from Apple Health if you turn that on. See Apple Health (iOS) below.
  • Error reports: anonymous crash and error data via Sentry, used to fix bugs. No personal identifiers beyond a randomly-generated session ID.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or social-graph data.
  • No third-party analytics trackers (no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.).

Where it's stored

  • Account + training data: Supabase (PostgreSQL, US region).
  • Static site hosting: Cloudflare Pages.
  • AI training-plan generation: The app sends your setup inputs (name, goal, experience, days per week, equipment) to a Cloudflare Worker, which calls Anthropic's Claude API to generate your program. No identifying information beyond what you enter in the setup form is sent.
  • Error logs: Sentry (anonymized).

What we never do

  • Sell your data.
  • Share your data with third-party advertisers.
  • Use your training data to train AI models.

Apple Health (iOS)

Apple Health integration is off until you turn it on in Settings → Apple Health inside the app. Turning it on covers two things, and iOS asks your permission for each separately — you can allow one and refuse the other.

  • Body weight, read: we read your most recent body weight so the figure shown in your profile and workout logs stays current without you re-entering it.
  • Body weight, written: when you log a body weight in The Foundry, we save that reading back to Apple Health so it stays in sync with your other health and fitness apps.
  • Workouts, written: when you finish a session, we save it to Apple Health as a strength-training workout — start and end time, an estimate of active energy burned, and a label identifying the training day. This is what makes the session appear in Apple Fitness and count toward your Activity rings.

The energy figure is an estimate calculated on your device from the session's duration and your body weight. If you wear an Apple Watch, its own measurement is more accurate and takes precedence.

We do not read your workouts, heart rate, steps, sleep, or any other health data. Nothing we write to Apple Health leaves your device by way of Apple Health — the workouts and weights we save there are stored by Apple under your control, not sent to us through it.

Health data read from Apple Health is stored the same way as the rest of your training data (see Where it's stored) and is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train AI models. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → The Foundry.

Your rights

  • Access: email us and we'll send you an export of your data.
  • Deletion: in-app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account — this permanently deletes your account and all associated training data. You can also email us to request the same.
  • Correction: you can edit all profile and training data from within the app.

Children

The Foundry is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If we learn we have collected such data, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be communicated in-app.
