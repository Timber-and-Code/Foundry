## Description (4000)

The Foundry builds your strength program and runs it with you, week by week.

Tell it how many days you train, what equipment you have and how long you want the block to run. The Foundry builds a full mesocycle: the right exercises for your split, sets and reps that climb from week to week, and a deload at the end so you come back stronger. Review every day before it starts. Swap any lift, add or remove exercises, then train exactly what you approved.

BUILT LIKE A REAL PROGRAM
• Periodized mesocycles of 4, 6, 8 or 12 weeks, plus a deload
• Volume that ramps from week to week, with reps in reserve (RIR) targets on every set
• Anchor lifts that carry your progress from one block to the next
• Full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs or push/pull splits, 2–6 days a week
• Coach-tune your program before you start, or build every day by hand
• 249 exercises across barbell, dumbbell, cable, machine, kettlebell, band and bodyweight. Pick "All equipment" for a full gym

TRAIN, DON'T GUESS
• Every set starts from what you did last week, so you always know what to lift
• Rest timer with a chime when it's time to go
• Swap an exercise mid-workout, for one day or the whole block
• Supersets, warm-up sets and bodyweight movements handled properly
• Deload weeks that actually back off

SEE WHAT THE WORK BUILT
• Exercise history with top weight, week by week
• An end-of-block summary: strength gained, sessions, PRs and volume by week
• Previous mesocycles, with lifts grouped by muscle
• Body weight trend

MORE THAN LIFTING
• Cardio plans: 7 protocols from easy walks to intervals
• 11 guided mobility routines, including pre-lift warm-ups and cool-downs
• Daily readiness check-ins

TRAIN WITH FRIENDS
• Add friends with an invite code and follow each other's training
• Share a program and train the same block together
• Sharing is mutual, and you choose Full or Basic. Remove a friend any time

WORKS WITH APPLE HEALTH
• Finished workouts appear in Apple Fitness and count toward your Move ring
• Body weight syncs both ways
• Completely optional, and off until you turn it on

Your training is saved to your account and syncs across your devices. Delete your account and data at any time from Settings.
