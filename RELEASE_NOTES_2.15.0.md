# 2.15.0 — build 1

## Your workouts now reach Apple Fitness

Finish a session and it's saved to Apple Health as a real workout, so it shows
in Apple Fitness → Workouts and its energy counts toward your Activity rings.

Turn it on in **Settings → Apple Health**. iOS asks two separate permissions —
body weight and workouts — and you can allow one and refuse the other. Calories
are estimated on your device from session length and body weight; if you wear an
Apple Watch, its own measurement is more accurate and takes precedence.

## Bodyweight exercises finally progress

Inverted Row, dips, push-ups and everything else that carries no load were being
told to do *fewer* reps than you'd managed the week before. They now progress on
reps the way loaded lifts progress on weight, and a bodyweight movement reads
"BW" in the weight field instead of an empty box that looks like missing data.

Weighted and assisted variants — weighted pull-ups, assisted dips — keep
tracking their added or assisted load as before.

## Added and removed sets stick

Adding a fourth set used to last exactly one week before quietly reverting to
the prescribed three. Your choice now carries forward, and it rides on top of
the program rather than freezing it: when the block adds a set of its own, you
still get it. Remove a set any time and that becomes the new normal.

These choices now sync, so they survive a reinstall and follow you to a second
device.

### What to look at

- **Inverted Row on Full Body D** — check the suggested reps are at or above
  what you did last week, never below.
- **Add a set** to anything this week, then look at next week's session.
- **Apple Health** — turn it on, finish a workout, then open Apple Fitness and
  confirm the session is there with a sensible duration.
